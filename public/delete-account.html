⚠️ What happens when you delete your account?

In accordance with the Google Play Store Account Deletion Requirement and GDPR / CCPA privacy frameworks, you can unconditionally delete your account and all associated information at any time.

  • Profile & Credentials: Your username, email address, phone number, and encrypted password hash are permanently destroyed.
  • Leaderboard & Elo: Your rank, trophy count, win streaks, and career statistics are wiped from the public leaderboard.
  • Match Records: All past match histories, tokens captured, and score logs are deleted.
  • Social & Teams: Your profile is removed from your friends' contacts lists, and any team or clan associations are removed.
  • Retention Period: Deletions take effect immediately upon execution. No residual copies are retained.

🗑️ Request Permanent Account Deletion

Enter your login credentials to immediately and permanently destroy your account and all stored records.

If you forgot your password or cannot access the app, enter your registered email address or username. A verification ticket will be logged and the account will be deleted within 24 hours.

📱 Clear Device & Offline Storage

If you played in Guest Mode or offline on this device, your preferences and local match saves are saved in your browser's LocalStorage.

📲 How to Delete Directly Inside the Android / PWA App

You can also perform this directly inside the installed application:

  1. Open Ludo Game & Multiplayer Arena.
  2. Tap the Career tab in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Data Security card.
  4. Click Delete Account & Data and confirm.
