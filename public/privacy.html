🌐 Google Advertising & Active Internet Delivery

Active Internet Ad Delivery: When your device connects to an active internet connection, Ludo Game & Multiplayer Arena displays advertisements provided by Google (including Google AdSense, Google AdMob, and the Google Ad Network).

Offline Play Pause: When offline, ad serving is completely paused and zero network ad requests are made.

Google uses cookies or mobile advertising IDs to serve ads based on your visits to our game and other internet sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising anytime by visiting Google Ads Settings.

1. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization. You can play Ludo Arena anonymously as a guest without creating an account or providing any personal information.

  • Optional Account Registration: If you choose to register, we collect a username, optional email address, optional country/flag, and a securely salted PBKDF2 hash of your password. We never store plain text passwords.
  • Local Storage (LocalStorage & IndexedDB): Your theme customization, game audio settings, active offline match progress, and guest career stats are stored locally on your device.
  • Multiplayer Match Data: When playing online matches, dice rolls, token positions, match outcomes, and quick-chat messages are transmitted over secure WebSocket connections to sync gameplay between players.
  • No Sensitive Financial or GPS Data: We never collect bank accounts, credit cards, or fine GPS location coordinates.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide real-time online multiplayer matchmaking and turn synchronization.
  • To maintain global and regional championship Elo leaderboards.
  • To enable cross-device account recovery and save game state.
  • To serve non-intrusive advertisements when connected to the internet.

We do NOT sell, lease, or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Account Deletion & Data Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data Policy, GDPR (Article 17), and CCPA, you have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

You can delete your account directly inside the app under Career / Profile ➔ Delete Account & Data, or by using our dedicated external web deletion tool:

Go to Account Deletion Request Page

Upon confirmation, your username, email, phone number, password hash, match history, Elo rating, and leaderboard records are permanently and irrecoverably erased from our production databases.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Ludo Game & Multiplayer Arena is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us and we will delete the data immediately.

5. Security & Data Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) and Secure WebSockets (WSS). Passwords are encrypted using salted PBKDF2 with SHA-512 hashes.
